# Disclaimer

This software is an early research scaffold for presenting oil-like slick
candidates to human reviewers. It is not an automatic oil-spill confirmation
system and is not production-ready.

Outputs may be incomplete, incorrect, delayed, or caused by look-alikes such as
low wind, natural films, rain cells, currents, or imaging effects. An
`uncertain` or `likely slick` label is a review state, not official ground
truth. A review priority is not a statement about severity.

Do not use this software alone for navigation, emergency response, enforcement,
vessel attribution, regulatory reporting, legal conclusions, or public
allegations. Any operational use requires independent evidence, qualified manual
review, documented provenance, suitable security controls, and validation for
the intended setting.
