# Code of Conduct

Project spaces must remain safe and useful for technical work. Treat other
contributors with respect. Discuss the work rather than a person's identity,
background, or motives. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted
sexual attention, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained
personal attacks are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these rules and may
restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately to a
maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
Include only the information needed to assess the report. Maintainers should
protect the reporter's privacy, avoid conflicts of interest, and explain the
outcome when it is safe to do so.
